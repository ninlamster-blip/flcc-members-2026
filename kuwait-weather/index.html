        Where this comes from. Forecast and air quality from Open-Meteo, which is free, keyless and open — weather from national meteorological services, dust and particulates from the Copernicus atmosphere model. Nothing about you is sent anywhere: the app asks for a set of coordinates and gets a forecast back.

        Where it stops. This is not an official warning service. Kuwait's Meteorological Department issues those. Heat index and WBGT are calculated from the forecast, not measured, and the work/rest guidance is for planning a day rather than for meeting an obligation. The 11:00–16:00 summer ban is the standing annual rule — confirm the current year's decree with the Public Authority for Manpower.

        Everything this app remembers — your place, your units, your work profile and the last reading — stays in this browser under kw/v1/.
