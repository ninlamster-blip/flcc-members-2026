      How this works

      The grid

      Tap a square to select it. Tap it again to switch between Across and Down. Type to fill it in, backspace to go back, arrow keys to move. A word turns green once every letter of it is right.

      The clues

      They are deliberately hard, and aimed at people who already know the famous stories. When one defeats you, work down the help: a hint, then the Scripture reference, then a single letter. Each costs points; none of them ends the puzzle.

      Scoring

      Correct answer +100. Finishing +500. Finishing with no hints at all +500 more, and a further +1,000 if you also never filled a word in wrongly. Hint −50, Scripture −100, revealed letter −150, Ask AI −100.

      Your streak

      One puzzle finished on a day keeps the streak alive. Everything is kept on this device only — no account, nothing uploaded.
