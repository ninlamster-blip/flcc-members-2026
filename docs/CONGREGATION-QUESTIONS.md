# Sharing our church app with 11 other churches — what we need to agree on first

Our members' app has been running for FLCC – Abundance Church. We have been
asked to open it up to 11 more churches. Before we do, there are decisions
that are not technical — they belong to the congregation and to the leaders
of each church. Please read this and tell us what you think.

## 1. The most important question: our information is currently public

Right now the app has **no password**. Anyone who has the link — or who finds
it through Google — can open it and read:

- the names of all 68 workers and members on the schedule
- 37 birthdays and 29 wedding anniversaries
- who is assigned to which service, on which date
- **attendance records** — who was present and who was absent, by name, for
  every service we have recorded
- the ministry head's personal phone, WhatsApp and email address

This is true today, for us. If we add 11 churches, it becomes true for roughly
800 people.

**We need to ask each of you plainly:**

- Are you comfortable with your name, birthday, anniversary and service
  assignments being on a page anyone in the world can open?
- Are you comfortable with **your attendance** — present or absent — being
  visible that way?
- Would you rather the app asked for a password before showing any of this?

A password would mean one extra step every time you open the app. That is the
trade. We would like the congregation to choose it, not us.

If even a few members are uncomfortable, our recommendation is to put member
information behind a password before any other church joins.

## 2. Who will look after each church's information?

Each church needs one or two people who:

- keep the schedule and member list up to date
- publish changes when they happen
- are the person to approach if something is wrong

**Who will that be in your church?** We need a name before we can set you up.

## 3. Should the prayer chain be shared between all 12 churches?

The app has an anonymous prayer chain. Someone posts a request; others tap to
say they have prayed for it.

Each church will run its own copy of the app, so member lists and schedules stay
separate by default. The prayer chain is the exception: it uses a shared
database, so if every church points at the same one, **everyone would see
everyone's prayer requests** — one wall across all 12 churches — and a new
request would notify members of all 12.

There is a real choice here:

- **Shared:** 12 churches interceding for each other. Beautiful, but your
  request is read by strangers from other churches.
- **Private per church:** only your own church sees your requests.

**Which would you prefer?** We can build either, but we should decide on
purpose rather than by accident.

## 4. Which parts does your church actually want?

The app has grown to around 20 different sections — service schedule,
attendance, prayer, music ministry, Bible study tools, reading plans, and
several games and learning tools.

No church has to take all of it. **Tell us which parts are useful to you** and
we will show only those. A shorter app is an easier app.

## 5. Who pays, and what happens if that person stops?

Two things cost real money once 12 churches are using them:

- the AI assistant ("Ask") — billed per question asked, by anyone, with no
  limit currently in place
- the hosting and database

Right now this sits with one person. With 12 churches it needs an agreed
arrangement: who pays, how much is reasonable, and what happens to everyone's
data if that person can no longer continue. We should not skip this
conversation.

## 6. How does someone get their information removed?

If a member asks to be taken off the app — for any reason, and without having
to explain — we need an agreed way to do that quickly, in every church. Who do
they ask, and how fast should it happen?

## What happens next

The technical setup is ready — each church can be given its own copy of the app,
already emptied of Abundance's member information, with instructions at
`docs/NEW-CHURCH-SETUP.md`.

What is not settled is section 1. **No church should put its members' details
in until its own congregation has been told the site is public and has agreed.**
Sections 2 and 3 we need answers to before a church goes live; sections 4 to 6
can follow.

Please send your answers, questions and concerns to the Fellowship Ministry.
Every objection is welcome — especially about section 1.
